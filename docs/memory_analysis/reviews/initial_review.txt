# Independent initial ACL critique

Verdict: REVISE the analysis; REJECT the current novelty claims. The available results can fill 2.5 ACL pages with careful, useful system analysis. They do not yet justify 2.5 pages presented as newly discovered properties of agent memory. A stronger method-specific result remains possible, but should depend on the matched existing-method control below. Do not assume it will succeed.

Scope: read story.md lines 1–100, algorithm.md, and relevant current and historical results.md sections, plus primary papers. No repository edits, jobs, model calls, new datasets, or scoring changes were made for this review. Existing results are documented test-as-dev, seed 42; four readers share one-shot source selections.

## 1. The principal novelty collisions

- MQuAKE already establishes that recalling an edited fact does not entail correctly answering questions whose answers change as a consequence of that edit; MeLLo uses externally stored edits during multi-step answering. Thus “a factual correction changes which other memory is relevant” is the benchmark's premise, not a new discovery. The Dubliners intervention is useful evidence of where this particular implementation succeeds, not conceptual novelty. https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.971/
- MemoryAgentBench constructs FactConsolidation from MQuAKE counterfactual pairs, orders replacements after originals, and explicitly instructs final-state reasoning. Appendix K even reports that stronger overwrite prompting does not fix multi-hop update propagation. Both FC variants constitute one update family. https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05257
- HippoRAG 2 already combines query-to-triple recognition, phrase and passage seeds, source passages, and PPR; its appendix distinguishes fact-recognition and subsequent source-selection failures. Calling these operations context recommendation does not create a new mechanism. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/gutierrez25a.html and https://arxiv.org/html/2502.14802
- CatRAG explicitly targets partial versus complete evidence, drift into generic hubs, query-dependent edge weighting, and support-bearing entity–passage edges. Its §3.4 boosts passage edges supported by recognized facts; §6 analyzes hub bias. Thus “links exist but weights prevent complete evidence” also overlaps directly. The potential distinction is the exact fixed, offline weight construction, compared against its actual published/released alternative, not the word recommendation. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.290.pdf
- AnchorMem already separates atomic fact anchors from immutable original context, reconstructs original context through fact/source links, and uses event associations to avoid misleading generic-entity bridges. “Facts locate memories while sources preserve meaning” substantially restates its central contribution. Its ablations remove raw context and event associations. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1736.pdf and https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17377
- LightMem §5.6 explicitly warns that related but noncontradictory entries can be mistaken for conflict, causing deletion of useful historical information. It motivates delayed updates partly through this failure. Nate's removed tournament event is an excellent implementation diagnosis, but does not establish a new state-versus-history principle. https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18866
- A-MEM already evolves historical notes and their links when new information arrives. It is an important collision with any broad claim about adaptation of associations; it is not evidence of AMOR's online capability. https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12110v4
- LongMemEval explicitly separates updates, temporal reasoning, multi-session reasoning, and abstention, and investigates the separation between memory construction, selection, and reading. Zep preserves historical relationships; APEX-MEM uses append-only history and query-time conflict resolution. Do not present the need to preserve historical evidence while selecting currently applicable information as newly identified. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13956 ; https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.749.pdf

## 2. What the current evidence actually establishes

The strongest observation is the controlled 2Wiki improvement, not the rhetorical separation between search and recommendation. At identical topology, recognized facts, initial scores, PPR, fusion and top-five budget, complete support rises from 635 to 689 of 1,000 questions: 56 complete gains and 2 losses. Of the gains, 32 are bridge-comparison questions. This is specific and useful evidence that the implemented weights alter competition among already connected sources.

However, the claimed exclusivity of indirect evidence gains has a severe ceiling problem. In the recognized-fact population, unit weights already select 1,259 of the 1,260 gold occurrences available in recognized facts' retained sources. There is only one possible direct-source improvement. The 88 indirect gains / 5 indirect losses and 0 direct changes are accurate, but “all gains are indirect” is not by itself surprising. Report the denominators alongside the counts. Do not treat the direct and indirect groups as exchangeable populations.

A second alternative explanation is ordinary removal of noisy associations. Under fixed AMOR query components, original HippoRAG has Recall/Complete 74.825/46.8; simply removing existing synonym edges gives 86.200/66.7; AMOR gives 87.400/68.9. Most of the headline gap is already reproduced by this named edge deletion. Binary AMOR topology gives 84.775/63.5. The weight contribution is real relative to that topology, but the comparison against the strongest existing control is only 1.2 Recall points and 2.2 Complete points. These are not additive causal decompositions.

The Michael Curtiz intervention demonstrates a local ranking cause: weight 26 versus 1 changes entry into the five-source list. It does not establish that 50 extracted facts are independent corroboration, that the weight is calibrated to relevance, or that stronger semantic relations were discovered. A biography naturally contains many different facts about its central entity; document length and entity-focused content remain plausible explanations. Do not invent length-normalized weights or random counterfactual weights to manufacture a mechanism claim. First test the existing CatRAG alternative.

The full LoCoMo 2x2 is valuable because it isolates the implemented neighbor operation. With facts absent, neighbors raise single-hop F1 from 52.04/52.71/44.09/51.45 to 67.44/67.19/55.14/65.96 while lowering adversarial refusal from 61.43/70.63/41.26/57.40 to 42.60/65.92/19.51/39.01. The same operation has opposite effects under existing answerable and unanswerable task categories. This is operational specificity, not a newly discovered risk of misattribution. Nate supplies a traced example of source recovery after this method's own retention removed an extracted event; Melanie supplies a localized harmful example. Neither labels population error mechanisms.

The 104/2,345 native support-turn occurrences with all extracted facts removed and zero graph degree, of which 23 appear as centers and 55 with neighbors, diagnose access loss. The denominator is annotated turns, not answer facts or independent questions. This cannot be presented as the fraction of historical events wrongly consolidated.

FC candidate interventions are useful population controls. All versus retained recognition candidates lowers native EM from 14/16/10/6 to 11/12/9/3 on all 100 FC-MH questions. All versus retained augmentation candidates lowers FC-SH from 75/82/71/43 to 60/68/56/26 at fixed five texts and ten-fact budget. But these establish operation effects, not prevalence of update propagation or stale assertion reinstatement. FC-MH remains very weak in absolute terms. Witches' single-assertion removal/replacement/restoration is good local evidence; its appended position, salience and repetition are not separated. Do not elevate these to a general consistency mechanism.

MH-Doc's all-100 recommendation improvement extends system evidence beyond FC. The Kirton example diagnoses wrong entity/date association, but AMOR retains the distractor too. It does not show implemented qualification checking. The SH-Doc nephew example uses empty-recognition dense fallback and cannot substantiate graph recommendation.

## 3. Nature and coverage of the tasks

Use six evaluation settings, three benchmark suites, and distinguish four data origins: MemoryAgentBench's RULER document QA and MQuAKE-derived FC; LoCoMo conversations; the HippoRAG 1,000-question 2Wiki release. SH/MH variants do not become independent dataset families. All six settings need to remain in the native-metric comparison. The 2Wiki release is not the complete original 2Wiki dataset.

These tasks ask different things of memory: documentary association and dated/entity-specific evidence; explicitly instructed final-state update use; conversation history and speaker/event interpretation with abstention; and annotated multi-document support chains. The study concerns offline reusable context construction and selection for QA. It does not test procedural learning, online agent adaptation, or general agent memory. A unified claim must explain the differing outcomes rather than treating all six as votes for the same mechanism.

There is also a methodological tension with the user instruction against heuristic research inventions. “Latest source for each subject–relation” is licensed as an update ordering assumption by FC's explicit protocol. Applying it to a nonfunctional historical relation such as won is not justified by that protocol. The Nate result exposes this directly. Do not silently cure this with a new relation taxonomy, classifier, filtering rule, dataset relabeling, or handpicked subset. Report the operation accurately and ground any future replacement in an existing method before implementation.

## 4. Minimum discriminating experiment: released CatRAG key-fact operator

Question: Does AMOR's fixed weighting of all retained entity–source participation improve native evidence completeness beyond strengthening the source links of already recognized facts?

Published operator: CatRAG ACL §3.4 Eq. 3 multiplies a matching entity-to-passage edge by 1 + beta, and Table 7 sets beta=2.5 (therefore factor 3.5). Paper Table 5 already reports that removing passage enhancement improves 2Wiki Recall from 87.0 to 88.4. Reproducing that known sign alone is not a new finding.

Released local implementation differs. In /oscar/home/zliu328/agent-memory/baseline_algorithms/CatRAG/src/catrag/CatRAG.py:

- Lines 1825–1830 form recognized fact IDs.
- Lines 1832–1864 enumerate each recognized phrase's outgoing entity-to-passage edges and test overlap with those fact IDs.
- Line 1868 uses a factor of 2.5, not 3.5.
- Lines 1878–1894 rescale all that seed's passage-edge weights to preserve their original total, and modify only the forward arcs.
- Later code separately adds symbolic anchors and relation-edge adjustments; those must stay disabled in a key-fact-only test.

Use a clearly named released-code condition with factor 2.5 and its normalization as the primary code reproduction, documenting the paper discrepancy. A separate exact-equation condition is possible but must not be silently substituted or selected by results. Public source: https://github.com/kwunhang/CatRAG/blob/main/src/catrag/CatRAG.py (local revision is what was inspected).

Compare the existing binary AMOR graph, AMOR weights, and the released CatRAG key-fact operator applied to the binary graph. Optional fourth cell applies the same published operator to AMOR weights to check whether the effects coexist. Keep recognition, source graph, candidates, seeds, damping, fusion, budgets and source identifiers fixed. If AMOR is undirected, represent every original edge as reciprocal directed arcs for every condition and first verify unchanged baseline PPR and final selection; apply CatRAG's update only to entity-to-passage arcs. Symmetric boosting would be an invented variant. If exact correspondence cannot be established, report incompatibility instead of silently changing the operator.

Run all 1,000 2Wiki questions and report existing Recall/Complete and official-type results, plus current whole-population paired gains/losses. Keep the 56 fallback questions in primary totals and disclose the recognized subset separately. Native answer scores should test whether coverage gains survive reading; no new consistency metric or curated subset. For a new system comparison, retain all six settings using their native evaluators, although only datasets with official compatible source annotations can support passage metrics.

Interpretation is contingent: if CatRAG matches AMOR, the special-weight explanation weakens; if AMOR wins only on 2Wiki, state a dataset-specific advantage; if it produces robust gains across native tasks with a visible different selected-source pattern, that supports a specific construction choice over an established alternative. It still does not establish a new general principle of memory.

## 5. AnchorMem and presentation questions

A table of seven methods failing on one selected question does not explain why AMOR beats AnchorMem. Inspect the complete existing outputs for the same native questions: does its generated context omit the needed original material, retain it but lose attribution, or provide it and still receive a wrong reader answer? Use only available original source mappings and the native scores. Do not infer source coverage from a generated summary without a reliable mapping. AnchorMem's released no-raw-context ablation is the relevant published comparison if extending controls; “raw context helps” is already its claim.

The strongest immediate use of existing data is to assemble current, correctly versioned support-selection graph/candidate ablations and the LoCoMo presentation 2x2 into one native-task table. Do not merge older projection results with current simplified results. Population errors should remain unclassified unless the original dataset already supplies the relevant category. Existing case tracing can explain illustrative rows without fabricating a new annotation scheme.

## 6. Reviewable 2.5-page presentation if the discriminating control succeeds

Use one compact task-and-control table spanning all six settings, grouped by benchmark/data origin. Show counts, native metric, fixed components, result, and the supported limitation. Do not average unlike metrics or count four readers as four source-selection replications.

Use one figure comparing complete-support outcomes under binary weights, released CatRAG key-fact weights, and AMOR weights, split only by official 2Wiki types. Include the original-minus-synonym control so pruning's explanatory role is visible. Caption the direct-source ceiling explicitly. An adjacent LoCoMo panel can show the same neighbor operation's single-hop benefit and refusal loss, with four readers shown separately. This is empirical explanation, not a conceptual discovery graphic.

Use one compact example table with four native settings: 2Wiki weight intervention, FC-MH updated association, FC-SH stale source plus governing assertion, and LoCoMo Nate/Melanie benefit/harm. Each row should show the actual question, a short source excerpt with provenance, changed operation, omitted or restored information, native outcome and claim boundary. Full five-source lists can be an appendix. Avoid invented graph paths: the director is already a seed, and deleting the movie/director edge did not remove the biography.

Allocate roughly one page to the matched existing-method comparison and its native-task implications, three quarters to population presentation tradeoffs, half a page to source-verified examples, and the remainder to scope and boundaries. That would be a credible analysis section. Without the matched alternative and clear all-six versioned results, the section is a collection of competent ablations and known failure illustrations, not a strong novel account of why this method improves on the closest graph/memory algorithms.

No extra terminology, formal objective, theoretical guarantee, invented metric, heuristic control, synthetic assertion, or new curation is needed. The result of the CatRAG test should determine whether to strengthen or narrow the claim.
